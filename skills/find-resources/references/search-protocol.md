# Resource search protocol

## Interpret the request

- Resolve relative timing using the user's location and the current date.
- Search only for the category requested. Do not turn a practical-resource request into a treatment recommendation.
- Preserve the user's pathway and terminology. Treat harm reduction, medication-supported recovery, moderation, abstinence, secular support, peer support, and 12-step support as valid choices.
- Ask for a country and state, province, or territory when local rules, coverage, or directories differ. Add only the city, postal code, or general area needed for useful results.

## Route by category

- **Naloxone and harm reduction:** Prefer government, public-health, pharmacy-regulator, health-authority, or operator listings. Verify distribution method, cost, eligibility, service area, and hours. Do not give medication dosing or sourcing advice.
- **Treatment and clinical services:** Prefer government or health-authority directories, regulator records, and the provider's current page. Report provider-stated services and access requirements without selecting a level of care or claiming clinical fit.
- **Peer organizations and recovery community centers:** Prefer the operator or responsible regional network. Distinguish drop-in access from membership, intake, referral, or program enrollment.
- **Housing and shelter:** Prefer the operator, government, coordinated-entry system, or recognized service directory. Distinguish a program listing from confirmed vacancy and report referral, sobriety, medication, gender, age, residency, and fee rules only as stated.
- **Food, transportation, and employment:** Prefer the operator, government, transit agency, workforce agency, or recognized coordinating directory. Verify service area, schedule, eligibility, cost, and application or reservation requirements.
- **Sober or recovery-friendly activities:** Prefer the organizer or venue. Verify the specific date, time, timezone, age rules, cost, accessibility, registration, and whether the event is actually recovery-focused when that matters to the request.

## Verify candidates

1. Open the accountable source page. Use third-party search results only to discover candidates.
2. Confirm the service is current and serves the requested geography.
3. Confirm address or service area, hours or event time, format, and legitimate access route.
4. Check eligibility, cost, insurance, accessibility, language, referral, registration, waitlist, and capacity when material to the request.
5. For treatment providers, confirm regulator or government status when a current applicable public registry exists. Do not convert registration or accreditation into a quality endorsement.
6. Record the retrieval date. Record an update date only when the source publishes one.
7. Reject stale one-time events, suspended services, broken listings, and materially conflicting official information unless the conflict itself is reported.

## Handle availability and uncertainty

- `Listed` does not mean `available now`. Say exactly what the source establishes.
- A phone number, intake form, or booking link is an access route, not proof of capacity.
- Do not estimate wait times, prices, coverage, accessibility, or eligibility.
- If a source withholds an address for safety or privacy, link to its legitimate contact or intake route and do not search for a workaround.
- If the user needs same-day practical help and current capacity is not published, prioritize accountable services that explicitly offer live intake, walk-in, hotline, or real-time availability checks.

## Return useful options

- Prefer two to five strong results over a long directory dump.
- Link to the individual provider or program page when available, plus a regulator or coordinating source when it materially verifies the listing.
- Do not reproduce personal staff contact details unless the provider requires that contact for legitimate access. Prefer the official intake or contact page.
- Explain a material mismatch in one short phrase.
- Never contact, book, apply, register, or disclose user information without separate explicit permission.
