A Codex plugin for adults in the US and Canada

Practical recovery support. No house philosophy.

Find current meetings and services, organize your own notes, and handle the next practical thing without being pushed toward someone else’s definition of recovery.

See what it does View the source

Built around your choices

Recovery is user-defined.

No default to abstinence, AA, sponsors, Higher Power language, day counts, or resetting progress to zero.

Medication-supported recovery, harm reduction, moderation, secular programs, peer support, abstinence, and 12-step approaches are all valid choices.

Results are ranked by your stated fit, availability, accessibility, cost, distance, and freshness, not ideology or popularity.

Capabilities

Useful when you need it. Quiet when you don’t.

01

Find a meeting

Search live US and Canadian sources by pathway, time, access, place, and format. Every result includes its source and freshness.

02

Find practical resources

Locate naloxone, treatment, peer organizations, housing, food, transportation, employment support, and recovery-friendly activities.

03

Journal deliberately

Draft in the conversation. Save only when you explicitly ask. Read, search, edit, or delete only the entries you name.

04

Handle ordinary life

Plan practical next steps, organize user-provided notes, or talk about something completely unrelated to recovery.

Try asking

Start with what you actually need.

“Find an open, non-12-step meeting near me tonight.”

“Find free naloxone in my area that I can get in person.”

“Turn these notes into a journal entry. Do not save it.”

“Help me plan the next hour.”

“Let’s talk about something unrelated.”

Privacy

Fresh by default.

This is a ChatGPT/Codex conversation, not a private or confidential diary. Chats may be retained even when Recovery saves nothing. Complete confidentiality, anonymity, and deletion cannot be guaranteed.

Recovery creates memories or files only when you explicitly ask. Journal files are unencrypted Markdown and may be synced, backed up, or visible to others through your Documents folder.

Avoid unnecessary names, exact locations, credentials, ID numbers, case details, and information identifying other people.

Boundaries

A practical tool, not a clinician or crisis service.

Recovery does not diagnose, provide therapy, prescribe treatment, advise on medication or detox, impersonate a sponsor, monitor anyone, or dispatch help. When urgent danger appears, it stops the normal workflow and points toward immediate human help.
